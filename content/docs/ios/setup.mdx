### 1. Register your iOS app

Head over to the [Dynalinks Console](https://dynalinks.app/console) and register your app:

- **Bundle Identifier**: you'll find this in your **Xcode project settings**.
- **Team ID**: locate it in your [Apple Developer account](https://developer.apple.com/account/ios/profile/) under **Provisioning Profiles**.
- **App Store ID**: found in your app's App Store URL, e.g.:  
  `https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/yourapp/id**123456789**`

---

### 2. Add the Associated Domain

In Xcode, enable Universal Links by adding your Dynalinks domain under **Signing & Capabilities**:

1. Open your Xcode project.
2. Select your app target.
3. Go to the **Signing & Capabilities** tab.
4. Click **+ Capability** and add **Associated Domains**.
5. Add this domain: `applinks:your-app.dynalinks.app`

More info:  
[Apple’s official guide to Associated Domains](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/supporting-associated-domains#Add-the-associated-domains-entitlement-to-your-app)

---

### React Native: setting up Associated Domains

If you're building your app using **React Native**, you can still enable Universal Links with Dynalinks by adding the Associated Domains entitlement to your iOS app:

#### Setup with `react-native` and `react-native-config`

1. **Locate your Xcode project** in the `ios/` folder.
2. Open `YourApp.xcworkspace` in **Xcode**.
3. Select your app target, then go to the **Signing & Capabilities** tab.
4. Click **+ Capability** and add **Associated Domains**.
5. In the domain list, add:
   ```
   applinks:your-app.dynalinks.app
   ```

   Replace `your-app` with your actual subdomain registered on Dynalinks.

---

**Tip:** you can manage these domains via environment variables using [`react-native-config`](https://github.com/luggit/react-native-config) to differentiate between staging and production environments.

That's it. Dynalinks can now open your app through Universal Links.
